Privacy Policy - Missile Dodge

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Missile Dodge ("the app") is published by AARCHEE SHARMA and is made for players aged 7 and up, including children. We take children's privacy seriously. This policy follows the Google Play Families Policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA and GDPR-K.

Short version for parents

The app has no accounts, no chat, no social features, no in-app purchases and no links to other apps. It does not ask for or store a child's name, email, photos, contacts, microphone or location.

Data kept on the device

Best score, stars, flight count and settings are saved only on the device. We never receive them. Settings > Reset progress, or clearing the app data in Android settings, erases them.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded). Because the app is for children, ad requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from users under the age of consent, and ad content is limited to the G (General Audiences) rating. Personalized (interest-based) ads and remarketing are turned off. The app does not request the Android advertising ID permission. To deliver and secure ads, AdMob may process limited technical information such as IP address, device type, app version and whether an ad was shown. See Google's information at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Rewarded ads

Watching a rewarded ad is optional and only gives extra lives. The game works fully without it.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. The only third party in the app is Google AdMob, as described above.

Parental choices

Parents can ask us questions or request deletion of any information we may hold by writing to the email below. Because the app keeps its data only on the device, removing it from the device also removes the data.

Security

We keep data collection to a minimum so there is very little to protect or leak.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version on the store page and in the app.
